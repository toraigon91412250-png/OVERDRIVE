# Shiganshina and Warhammer update

The supplied `shiganshina_city_v4.html` architecture replaces the old city geometry. It retains 386 buildings, the original landmark architecture, street furniture and canals in 62 instance batches. The existing Maria perimeter and tall clock tower remain connected to the new district. The initial combat avenue, central square, three supply lots and clock-tower lot stay clear.

House collisions include oriented gable and hip roofs. The player can land on roofs and grapple the actual rendered surfaces. Static target batches use a cached 32m spatial index, with ordinary Three.js triangle intersection on candidates; 41 reference rays matched native results, including transformed roots. Invisible legacy geometry is excluded from anchors, obstruction checks and collisions.

The supplied WarHammerTitan_GroundedStrike_V7_Animated model is a 15m enemy in the 13-enemy raid and a selectable single-enemy encounter. Body normalization excludes the hammer; showcase crystal clusters are removed. Its authored Hammer_Smash clip follows a windup, physical hammer-head sweep and recovery. Aim locks during the windup. Only physical contact damages humans or transformed players, and world cover blocks it. The nape follows the imported head hierarchy. Front punches are resisted; flank strikes and counters during recovery do more damage. It can be killed with the existing human nape attack.

The street-side supply camps at [-20,146], [-270,-160] and [260,150] replenish gas, blades and spear ammunition when a human lands and stops. Small green radar squares mark nearby camps. Enemies occupy separate districts and spawn outside the new masonry. Existing transformation, hardening, weapon controls, final collapse/steam and replay remain integrated.

Validation used real Three.js geometry, animation and game-loop harnesses with a stub renderer. New checks cover startup, physical contact and dodges, cover, clear spawns, all three camps, ammunition-only resupply, roof landings, wire attachment, blade kills, victory and replay. Existing model, roster, blade-motion, enemy-duel, combat, assist, navigation, collision, preferences and battlefield checks passed. A separate offline Blender geometry render inspected the assembled district from the game camera; it is not browser/WebGL gameplay verification. Browser input feel and GPU performance have not been manually verified.
